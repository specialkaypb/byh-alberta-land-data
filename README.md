# BYH Alberta oil and gas layers (live)

Zoom-to-load Google Earth layers built from Alberta Energy Regulator open data.
The BYH Property Diagnosis Filestream links here. This branch is replaced every
month by one fresh commit; the tools and history live on `main`.

Source: Alberta Energy Regulator public spatial data (ST37, ST102, Enhanced
Pipeline, Abandoned Well Map, Revised Abandoned Well Locations). Positions are as
licensed or recorded by the AER and are not a legal survey.

Built: 2026-10-30
